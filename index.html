FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services, process, and policies.

01

What types of projects do you handle?

We handle a wide range of projects including residential, commercial, industrial, interior design, and renovation projects.
02

How long does a typical project take?

Timeline varies depending on the project scope and complexity. Residential builds typically take 8-14 months, while commercial projects range from 10-18 months. We always provide a detailed schedule before starting.
03

Do you provide design and build services?

Yes, we offer complete end-to-end design and build solutions. Our integrated team of architects, designers, and construction specialists handles everything from planning approvals to final handover.
04

How do you ensure quality in construction?

We enforce strict quality control measures at every stage. This includes using premium certified materials, employing experienced site engineers, conducting regular inspections, and adhering to strict building codes.
05

Can I make changes during the construction process?

Yes, modifications can be made through a formal change order process. We assess the impact of changes on the timeline and budget, discuss options with you, and proceed only after your written approval.
06

Do you help with permits and approvals?

Absolutely. We manage the entire documentation, application, and liaison process with local planning authorities and corporation bodies to obtain all necessary building permits and approvals.
